### Slide 7: Intelligent Traffic & Lead Distribution
#### **Fair-Share Load Balancing Meets Precision Routing**

For car malls and dealer groups, lead allocation is the #1 political and operational challenge. FormulaOS provides two intelligent routing modes:

#### 1. Direct Vehicle Matching Mode
- If a customer inquires about a specific listing (e.g., *2023 Toyota Corolla at Store 4*), the lead is routed deterministically to Store 4's sales team.

#### 2. "Modo Feirão" / Fair Event Lead Balancing (Patented Logic)
- Used during auto shows, promotional campaigns, or general mall traffic:
- **Algorithmic Fairness Formula:**
  $$\text{Priority} = \min(\text{Leads This Month}) \longrightarrow \min(\text{Recent Activity}) \longrightarrow \text{Active Store Filter}$$
- Dynamically distributes generic car seekers across mall tenants to guarantee equal opportunity and maximize collective ROI.

> **Speaker Notes:**
> "Whether managing 5 stores or 50 stores, the mall operator has full control. During major retail promotional events, you switch to 'Fair Mode' with one click, ensuring all participating dealerships receive a balanced flow of qualified opportunities."
